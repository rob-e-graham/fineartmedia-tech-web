Privacy Policy

Daily Grace is designed to keep your spiritual reflections private on your device.

You do not need an account to use Daily Grace. FAMTEC does not collect, sell or receive your reflections or Ask Scripture messages.

Private on-device intelligence

When installed, FAMTEC Mobile AI processes Ask Scripture messages and reflections on your device. SafeChat checks messages locally so the app can show immediate support information when appropriate. The text of these conversations is not sent to FAMTEC.

Optional downloads

The app may download optional model files, fonts or other app resources from third-party hosting services. Those services may receive routine technical information, such as an IP address and device request details. Daily Grace reflections and Ask Scripture messages are not included in these downloads.

Notifications and sharing

Daily scripture reminders are scheduled on your device at the time you choose. Daily Grace creates a verse image only when you tap Share; you decide which person or service receives it through Apple’s share sheet.

Daily Grace Shop

The Daily Grace Shop opens an external Square website. Information provided there is handled under Square’s and the shop’s privacy practices, not by the Daily Grace app.

Your controls

You can turn notifications off or change their time in Settings. You can erase Daily Grace’s locally stored data from Settings → Erase Daily Grace Data. The shared FAMTEC Mobile AI model is retained because other FAMTEC apps on the device may use it; it can be removed separately from the FAMTEC Mobile AI controls.
