Notifications

Open Daily Grace → Settings → Daily Scripture to enable reminders and choose a time. If reminders are disabled at the iPhone level, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Daily Grace.

FAMTEC Mobile AI

Daily Grace can share one private on-device FAMTEC model with other FAMTEC apps. If the model is already installed for Aura, Daily Grace should use that shared copy rather than downloading another model.

SafeChat

SafeChat is an on-device safety layer. It can surface immediate human support options when appropriate, but it is not an emergency service or a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
