The Story of the Moving Image

The Object Wall

One wall. A century of the moving image. Ask it anything.
Concept demo · a proposal by Fine Art Media · not official ACMI content

From floor to ceiling, the object wall gathers the machines that first taught pictures to move. Zoetropes and praxinoscopes, magic lanterns, Muybridge’s galloping horse, right through to the screens we carry today. One voice speaks for the whole wall. Ask it out loud or by text, in your language, answered only from ACMI’s own records.
