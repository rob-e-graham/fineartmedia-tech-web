Built on TRIBE’s own words

TRIBE has one core value: care. This is care at 3am, in a language the client chose, when the advisor who holds it is asleep in Melbourne.

Confidant, guide, enabler, organiser — and fact checker

Where it comes from

  • Past itineraries, and what clients actually said afterwards
  • Advisor knowledge — the things Kelly knows that aren’t written down anywhere yet
  • Site inspections and supplier debriefs
  • The Virtuoso preferred-partner network

Every recommendation on the previous panels is tagged to a source in TRIBE’s record. Nothing is generated from general knowledge of the city.

The fact checker, literally

TRIBE already describe themselves as their client’s fact checker. That is exactly what grounded retrieval is: every answer names the itinerary, debrief or supplier file behind it, and where the record is silent it says so and names the person who knows.

An advisor who cannot check an answer will not trust it twice. A client told something wrong about their own trip will not forgive it once.

Personal, because the record is

Known

  • Aisle seats, forward cabin
  • High floor, away from lifts
  • Late starts — no 7am departures
  • Shellfish allergy · no red meat
  • 30th anniversary, Friday

Remembered

Kyoto 2023: “Loved the private tea ceremony. Said sixty minutes felt rushed.”

So Friday’s palace visit was booked at ninety. The client never has to say it twice.

Never asked for

No preference form, no profile to complete. It is captured as a by-product of the work advisors already do.

The luxury is in not being asked again.

Security, at this level of service

Stays private

  • Identities and contact details
  • Passports, visas, medical, dietary
  • Commercial terms and rates
  • The preference record itself

Leaves, de-identified

  • The reasoning, with minimum context
  • Public facts: hours, weather, transit
  • Never a name against a preference

Never

  • Books, rebooks or cancels
  • Quotes a price or commits TRIBE
  • Trains a public model on client data
  • Answers when the record is silent
The luxury standard and the security standard are the same standard. Discretion is a condition of the relationship at this level. Anything that reads as a client’s private information being handled casually costs more than the technology will ever save.

Why Prague Castle

TRIBE’s own site names a gala at Prague Castle, with a 48-piece orchestra accompanying the Czech National Ballet, among Leon’s favourite programs. It makes a useful demonstration because it holds everything difficult about this business at once: a fixed immovable moment, a venue you do not control, international suppliers, a client with history, and two hundred details that all have to be right simultaneously.

If the idea holds here, it holds on a Virtuoso leisure booking. The reverse is not true.
