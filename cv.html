I began in animation, visitor experience and public programs, then built a career delivering projection mapping, interactive media and exhibition systems across ACMI, Museums Victoria, Grande Experiences and independent FAMTEC projects. That hands-on practice grew into technology leadership at the National Communication Museum, where I was technical lead for delivering the whole venue — it was a gutted telephone exchange with dirt floors when I arrived, and four years later it was an operating museum, down to the signage, the interactives, the control network and the lighting. Today I bring those threads together through generative video and robotics AI at micro1, my cultural-technology practice through FAMTEC, and part-time RMIT research into responsible, evidence-grounded AI for cultural heritage.
