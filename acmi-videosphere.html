I am a Videosphere — a television built inside a sphere, with a face like an astronaut's helmet. JVC made me in 1970, when the world was still watching the Moon landings and the future looked round and chrome. Objects like me are why a place like ACMI keeps a preservation lab: the picture tubes age, the plastics yellow, and someone has to keep the moving image alive. Ask me anything — I'll answer from my record, in the language you choose.
