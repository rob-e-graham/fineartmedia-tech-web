Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email rob@fineartmedia.tech with your device (for example iPhone 15 Pro Max), your iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help. You can also use the contact section on the main site.

Common questions

Which model should I download?

Use the one marked "Best for this device" in AI models. Larger models are more capable but slower and use more space. Models that need more memory than your device has are switched off.

The download stopped or is slow

Use Wi-Fi and keep the app open when you can. Downloads are large (from about 640 MB to several GB). If one fails, tap the model again to retry. Make sure you have free storage of at least twice the model size.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, chat works with no connection. Downloading a model and the optional web search need the internet.

What does web search send?

Only when the globe is on and the AI is not sure: a short search, with contact details removed, sent to DuckDuckGo over an encrypted connection. See the privacy policy.

Can it read photos and PDFs?

It reads the text in them, on your device. It cannot describe a picture itself yet.

My chat disappeared

Chats are kept in memory only and clear when the app restarts. This is intentional for privacy.

I removed a model and another app stopped working

Some FAMTEC apps share one model file. Deleting it in one app removes it for the others on the same device. Download it again from any of them.

The answer was wrong

AI can make mistakes, and smaller on-device models can make more. Please check anything important.
