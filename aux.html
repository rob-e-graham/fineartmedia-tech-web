What is this?

AUX.IO is a live demonstration of ARCHAI™ — sovereign AI infrastructure that lets museum objects speak for themselves using verified institutional data. No hallucination. No cloud APIs. Everything runs on a single computer.

Each object answers from its Obtext (Object Context) — a structured data format containing only verified metadata. When you ask something outside its record, it says so.

Obtext format

{ "identity": "1866 Proof Sovereign", "institution": "Museums Victoria", "registration": "NU 23511", "materials": "Gold, proof strike", "date": "1866", "maker": "Royal Mint, Melbourne", "significance": "Earliest proof sovereign...", "constraints": [ "I do not know about cryptocurrency", "I cannot discuss objects outside my record", "My knowledge ends at my last verified update" ] }
