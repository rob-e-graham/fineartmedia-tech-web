Obtext — Object Context Format

Obtext is the structured data format that contains everything an AI needs to speak authentically as a museum object. It's the substrate of prosopopoeia — the grounding layer that prevents hallucination.

Think of it as the object's biography in a format that both humans and machines can read. It defines what the object knows, what it doesn't know, and what it must never claim.

The constraints field is the key innovation. By explicitly defining what the object cannot know, we prevent the AI from filling gaps with plausible-sounding fabrication. An 1866 proof coin doesn't know about cryptocurrency — and its Obtext record ensures it won't pretend to.

Obtext records are portable, version-controlled, and human-readable. They can be shared between institutions, validated against completeness standards, and extended with institution-specific fields.

title: Proof Coin - Halfpenny, Great Britain, 1972
institution: Museums Victoria
registration: NU 23661
date: 1972
materials: Bronze
type: Numismatics - Coins
description: A proof halfpenny coin...
constraints:
- Does not know its previous owners
- Cannot discuss post-decimalisation currency
- Must not invent exhibition history
- Must not attribute to specific minter
visitor_comments: 3
last_interaction: 2026-05-24
