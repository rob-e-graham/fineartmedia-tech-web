What is this?

AUX.IO is a live demonstration of ARCHAI™ — sovereign AI infrastructure that lets museum objects speak for themselves using verified institutional data. No hallucination. No cloud APIs. Everything runs on a single Mac Studio in Melbourne.

Each object answers from its Obtext (Object Context) — a structured data format containing only verified metadata. When you ask something outside its record, it says so.

62 objects with verified images across Museums Victoria and The Metropolitan Museum of Art are currently live and conversational. Harvesters for the Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art, Rijksmuseum (Amsterdam), and Europeana (4000+ European institutions) are ready to expand the collection further.

Obtext format

{ "identity": "1866 Proof Sovereign", "institution": "Museums Victoria", "registration": "NU 23511", "materials": "Gold, proof strike", "date": "1866", "maker": "Royal Mint, Melbourne", "significance": "Earliest proof sovereign...", "constraints": [ "I do not know about cryptocurrency", "I cannot discuss objects outside my record", "My knowledge ends at my last verified update" ] }

Access methods

AUX.IO is access-method agnostic. The same object page works via NFC tag, QR code, hyperlink, or Bluetooth beacon. The object speaks regardless of how you arrived.
