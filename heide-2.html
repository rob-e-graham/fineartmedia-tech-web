Heide I is the weatherboard farmhouse where the story begins. In 1934 John and Sunday Reed made this old dairy their home, and around its kitchen table gathered a circle of young modernists. In 1946–47 Sidney Nolan painted his first Ned Kelly series here. This version answers with a fully on-device voice: Whisper listens, Piper speaks, and no audio ever leaves the machine.

This v2 uses the production path when it can reach the local engine: Whisper for listening and Piper for the voice, both open source and fully on-device, so no audio ever leaves the building. Voices can be swapped per object or gallery, or cloned from your own visitor-services actors (Coqui XTTS). If the local engine isn't running, it falls back to the browser's built-in speech.
