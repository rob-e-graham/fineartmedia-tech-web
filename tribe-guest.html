TRIBE tips
Timing
The Charles Bridge, properly
Impossible between 10am and 6pm. Go at 6.30am for the light, or after 10pm when it empties.
Thursday morning is your only genuinely free one.
Hidden
Vrtba Garden
A baroque terraced garden almost nobody finds, five minutes from your hotel. Unmarked door on Karmelitská.
Worth the detour
Strahov Monastery library
The Philosophical Hall is extraordinary. General admission only lets you look in from the doorway.
Interior access needs arranging in advance. Two days’ notice — say the word.
Practical
Taxis and trams
Use the hotel car or a ride app. Street taxis in the centre are the one thing Prague still does poorly.
Practical
November evenings
Around 4–8°C after dark and the castle courtyard is exposed. Coats over black tie on Thursday.

Where Praguers eat
Kantýna
A butcher’s canteen. No bookings, communal tables, exceptional beef. Loud and genuinely local.
The beef is the whole point, so this is one for Alexandra. Worth knowing before you go.
Quiet, excellent
La Dégustation Bohême Bourgeoise
Tasting menu built on 19th-century Czech cookbooks. Small room, long evening.
Needs booking well ahead. Tell your Event Manager and they’ll try.
Avoid
The Old Town Square terraces
Beautiful view, indifferent food, tourist pricing. Walk two streets further in any direction.
Crystal, the real thing
Moser
The Karlovy Vary house since 1857. Flagship on Staré Město. They handle shipping and the VAT refund properly.
The shops on Celetná sell to visitors. This is where the pieces in the castle came from.
Made, not bought
A Bohemian workshop, outside the centre
TRIBE has commissioned gifts here for two previous programs. Needs a call ahead and about half a day.
If you’d like something engraved for the anniversary, Friday is the last day that works.
Garnets
Granát Turnov
Czech garnet is the local stone and heavily faked. This is the cooperative that actually mines it.
Books & prints
Antikvariát Karel Křenek
Old maps, prints and first editions. The kind of shop you lose an hour in.
