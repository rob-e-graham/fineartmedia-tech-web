FAMTEC Local AI: Privacy Policy

FAMTEC Local AI ("the app") is published by Rob Graham trading as FAMTEC, Melbourne, Australia. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app has no account, no analytics and no advertising network, and the conversations you have with it stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats. Messages and answers are held in the app's memory and cleared when the app restarts. They are never sent to us or to any server we run. We do not run a server that receives chats.
  • Photos, PDFs and files you add. The text is read on your device using Apple's text recognition. The photo or file is not uploaded.
  • Your personal settings. If you add a name, a response style or instructions, they are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are only used to shape the AI's replies on your device. You can clear them in Settings, and deleting the app removes them.
  • The AI model. The model is a file stored on your device. It may be kept in a shared folder (an Apple App Group) so other FAMTEC apps on the same device can use the same file. That folder contains the model file only, not your chats.

When the app uses the internet

  • Downloading a model. When you choose to download a model, your device connects to Hugging Face to fetch an open-source model file. Hugging Face and its content network receive the usual connection details such as your IP address. We receive nothing.
  • Web search, only if you switch it on. When the globe in the chat is on and the AI is not sure of an answer, the app sends a short search to DuckDuckGo's Instant Answer service over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The app removes email addresses, phone-style numbers, ID-style numbers and links from the search first, and keeps no cookies. DuckDuckGo receives the search and your IP address, under its own privacy policy. When the globe is off, nothing is sent.
  • Links. Tapping a link such as "Advertise here" opens a web page in your browser, which then follows that website's own policies.

Permissions

  • Camera is used only when you tap "Take photo", to read text from the picture on your device.
  • Photos are accessed only for the picture you choose.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information through the app, and we do not require an account.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, share or use your conversations for marketing or to train models.

Safety feature

Before each message reaches the AI, an on-device check looks for signs of crisis or distress and may show support services. This check runs entirely on your device and nothing about it is sent anywhere. It is not a medical or emergency service. In an emergency call your local emergency number (000 in Australia).

Your choices

  • Delete any model in the app's AI models screen. Deleting the shared model also removes it for other FAMTEC apps on the same device.
  • Clear your name, style and instructions in Settings.
  • Turn web search off at any time with the globe button.
  • Delete the app to remove its data from your device.

Accuracy

AI can be wrong. The app is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the contact details on fineartmedia.tech or the support page.
