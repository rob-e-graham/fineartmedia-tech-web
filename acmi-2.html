The Story of the Moving Image

The Object Wall

One wall. A century of moving images. Ask it anything.
Concept demo · a proposal by Fine Art Media · not official ACMI content

From floor to ceiling, the object wall gathers the machines that first taught pictures to move — zoetropes and praxinoscopes, magic lanterns and Muybridge’s motion studies, the spinning drums and mirror-toys of pre-cinema, through to contemporary works that spin them anew. Rather than a label on every case, one AUXIO speaks for the whole wall. Ask it aloud or in text, in the language you choose, grounded only in ACMI's records.
