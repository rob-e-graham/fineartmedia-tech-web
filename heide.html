Heide I — The Cottage
Farmhouse · c.1870, acquired 1934 · Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung Country
Heide Museum of Modern Art · Bulleen, Naarm/Melbourne
Public-domain image Victorian Heritage Register Concept — not official Heide content
Heide I is the original weatherboard farmhouse where the whole story begins. In 1934 the art patrons John and Sunday Reed bought this old dairy farm on the Yarra and made the cottage their home for the next thirty-five years. Around its kitchen table they gathered a circle of young modernists, and in 1946 to 47 Sidney Nolan painted his first Ned Kelly series right here. This small house is where Australian modernism took root.
