ARCHAI

Augmented Reanimation for Cultural Heritage and Artificial Intelligence — a local-first AI infrastructure framework designed specifically for GLAM institutions.

ARCHAI rejects dependency on cloud platforms, instead deploying sovereign inference servers, vector databases, and semantic collections within the institutional boundary. Each collection object gains its own dedicated AI instance, accessible via NFC-triggered conversational terminals.

Presented at ISEA 2026, Dubai. PhD research at RMIT University, School of Design.

Data Sovereignty
All inference and storage local to the institution. No external API dependency.
Curatorial Authority
Semantic collections governed by curators, not algorithms.
Object Intelligence
Per-object Ollama instances with hallucination validation layers.
Living Archive
Collections that grow through visitor interaction and institutional knowledge.

Strategic technology leader and creative technologist with over 20 years' experience at the intersection of art, digital infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and cultural heritage. I specialise in large-scale media systems, immersive environments, AI-integrated collections infrastructure, and exhibition technology.

At the National Communications Museum I led the full technical transformation of a heritage 1930s telephone exchange into a functioning contemporary technology museum — designing all digital infrastructure, delivering immersive exhibitions integrating XR and projection mapping, and securing over $100,000 in hardware savings through industry partnerships.

My independent practice spans immersive projection environments, interactive installations, extended reality works, and experimental AI-driven art systems. Collaborators include ACMI, Museums Victoria, MIFF, Heide Museum of Modern Art, and TarraWarra Museum of Art. My artistic inquiry explores the Hyper Sublime, techno-shamanism, simulation and digital ecologies, and post-religious transcendence in screen culture.

Current PhD research at RMIT develops ARCHAI — a sovereign AI infrastructure framework returning computational autonomy to GLAM institutions. The core proposition: AI belongs in museums, but on the institution's terms, not the cloud's.

Professional Vision

To build sovereign, preservation-aware, AI-integrated cultural infrastructure that allows institutions to maintain autonomy while expanding interpretive depth, access, and public engagement.
